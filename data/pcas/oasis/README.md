# OASIS PCA database

Head-shape basis built from 316 healthy subjects of the Open Access Series of
Imaging Studies (OASIS-1), aged 18 to 96. US/Western adult sample.

`../oasis_hartmut/` is the same 316 subjects with the neck-extended HArtMuT
scalp (2222 vertices instead of 1922); skull, CSF and cortex are unchanged.

Source: https://sites.wustl.edu/oasisbrains/

```
Marcus, D. S., Wang, T. H., Parker, J., Csernansky, J. G., Morris, J. C.,
and Buckner, R. L. Open Access Series of Imaging Studies (OASIS): Cross-
sectional MRI data in young, middle aged, nondemented, and demented older
adults. Journal of Cognitive Neuroscience 19, 1498-1507 (2007).
```
